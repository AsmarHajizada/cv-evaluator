Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Fennwick Mobility (Berlin, hybrid)

About us
Fennwick Mobility builds routing and pricing software for urban delivery fleets. We are a team of 60 and growing.

What you will do
- Design, train, and ship ML models that forecast demand and optimise fleet allocation.
- Deploy models to production and own their reliability, monitoring, and retraining.
- Work closely with data engineers and product managers to turn business problems into ML solutions.

What we are looking for
- 4+ years of experience building machine learning systems in industry.
- Strong Python and experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Production model deployment experience (APIs, containers).
- Hands-on experience running workloads on Kubernetes.
- Experience with MLOps practices: CI/CD for models, monitoring, and automated retraining.
- Solid SQL skills.
- Experience with time-series forecasting.

Nice to have
- Experience with LLMs or retrieval-augmented generation.
- Experience with AWS or GCP.
- MSc or PhD in a quantitative field.
